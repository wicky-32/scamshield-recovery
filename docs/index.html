Welcome

ScamShield is being developed to help people organize information about suspected scams and prepare reports for the relevant financial institution or authority.

Important information

Prototype only: This is an early demonstration. Do not submit real names, phone numbers, account details, passwords, transaction references, or other personal or financial information.

ScamShield cannot reverse payments, recover funds, freeze accounts, access bank or mobile-money systems, or guarantee recovery. Contact your bank, mobile-money provider, or appropriate authorities directly to report suspected fraud.

Development status

Secure reporting and case-tracking features are not yet available. They will only be introduced after security, privacy, and operational checks.
