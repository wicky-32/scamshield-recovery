Welcome to ScamShield Recovery

ScamShield Recovery is a prototype designed to help people document suspected scams, organize information and prepare reports for the appropriate institutions.

Important: ScamShield cannot recover money, reverse transactions, freeze accounts or access bank or mobile-money systems. Only authorized financial institutions and relevant authorities can investigate and take those actions.
