Welcome to ScamShield Recovery

ScamShield helps people document suspected fraud, organize available evidence and prepare information for reporting to the appropriate institution or authority.

Important: ScamShield is an independent prototype. It cannot freeze accounts, reverse M-Pesa or bank transactions, access financial institution systems, or guarantee recovery of money.

How it works

  1. Create an account or sign in.
  2. Submit a suspected scam report using test information.
  3. View your submitted reports and case references.
  4. Authorized administrators can review reports and update their recorded status.

Use fictional information only while this prototype is being tested.
